The method behind it

The questions the best decision science asks — asked every time.

Our proprietary 12-stage methodology compresses the discipline of elite decision analysis into days. Six moves carry the weight:

Frame

Name the real decision

The question as asked is rarely the question that matters. We structure the real one, with the constraints that actually bind it.

Widen

Surface every live option

Most bad decisions were between two options when there were five. We widen the set before anything gets scored.

Ground

Evidence, cited

Claims are tied to documents, data, and named sources — including your own financials, read and ratio-checked.

Score

Same terms, every option

Weighted criteria, dollar outcomes, probabilities — and simulation to show how robust the leader really is.

Attack

Try to kill the answer

Pre-mortem, adversarial re-scoring, and the outside view: how decisions like this one actually go, per the published research — before you commit, not after.

Commit

Then stay on it

KPIs, kill criteria, and a living risk register — monitored on a cadence, with a clear update in your hands every month.
