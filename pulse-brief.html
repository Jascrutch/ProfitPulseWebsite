Nov 27 · The AI arms race
Consulting & AI

Every consulting platform now ships an AI copilot. None of them show the client anything verifiable.

Kantata launched an “AI Superagent” in June; the rest of the professional-services software market followed with copilots of their own. All of it accelerates the consultant — none of it gives the client a way to check what the AI produced. Speed without verification just makes the wrong answer arrive sooner. Source →
Nov 27 · The hour is cornered
Consulting Economics

AI is forcing the biggest firms to rethink hourly fees. They built the model; the model is breaking.

When AI compresses months of analysis into days, a fee built on hours has a problem: the better the tools get, the less there is to bill. The industry’s answer so far is to protect the hour. Ours is to price the decision — the thing that was always worth the money. Source →
Nov 27 · The real deadline
Decision Timing

Typical engagements still run three to six months. The window to act is usually shorter than that.

Industry sources put the standard advisory engagement at months, with transformations running far longer. The deadline that matters isn’t the firm’s timeline — it’s how long the decision stays available. If the answer lands after the window closes, precision didn’t help. Source →
