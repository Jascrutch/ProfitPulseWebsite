The founders

Who you work with.

JC
Jason P. Crutchfield
Founder & CEO

Jason brings more than three decades of senior leadership and structured decision-making to Profit Pulse. He holds an Executive MBA from Duke University's Fuqua School of Business and a B.S. from the University of Alaska Anchorage, and served with the 2nd Battalion, 75th Ranger Regiment — where he developed the operational discipline and mission focus that anchor his approach to every engagement.

His career spans senior program leadership across major federal and defense programs, including a $60M+ aviation ISR portfolio supporting globally deployed aircraft, with deep expertise in earned value management (EIA-748), master scheduling, and the rigor required to keep complex, high-stakes programs on cost, on schedule, and defensible. He is a recognized EVMS subject-matter expert, is Six Sigma certified, holds active DoD and DoE security clearances, and is the author of Space Debris: Clearing the Final Frontier.

SY
Stephanie Yarnell, MD, PhD
Co-Founder & Chief Medical & Innovations Officer

Stephanie co-founded Profit Pulse and guides how the firm evaluates innovation, evidence and risk. She holds an MD and a PhD from the University of Florida and an Executive MBA with Honors from Duke University's Fuqua School of Business, and completed psychiatry fellowship training at Yale. She serves as Global Medical Director for New Products and Innovation at Sanofi's Vaccines Division, bringing an evidence-first, scientifically rigorous perspective to every engagement.
