Two ways we work with you

Decision consulting, and the layer that keeps it right.

Decision Consulting

The hard call, made clear.

When the stakes are high and the path isn't obvious, we give you a structured, defensible answer — fast. We frame the real decision, surface every viable option, stress-test the assumptions, and score each path on the same terms.

  • A clear recommendation, not a menu of options
  • Every claim traceable to evidence
  • Sensitivity analysis — what has to be true for it to work
  • Delivered in days, not weeks — as a concise report, not a hundred-page deck
  • Committed KPIs and kill criteria — in writing, so you know how to tell if it's working
Pulse Monitor

The decision, kept right.

A decision that's right today can drift as conditions change. Pulse Monitor tracks the indicators behind your recommendation and tells you when something moves enough to matter — so you adjust early, not after the fact.

  • Ongoing tracking of the indicators that drive your decision
  • Alerts when something shifts enough to act on
  • Three cadences — Essential, Active, Strategic
  • A monthly Pulse Update: readings, trends, risks, and what changed in the world around you
  • A Value Realization Statement on request — committed vs. measured, in your numbers
  • The big firms present and leave; we stay on the decision
Pricing that shares the risk

Priced to your decision — never by the hour.

There's no meter running and no reason for us to go slow. Every engagement is priced to your decision: what's riding on it, how many options are in play, and how deep the work needs to go. We scope that with you and agree the investment before we start — and if the work grows, we agree the change with you, so you're never surprised by an invoice. Where the decision suits it, we also offer an outcome-based option: a lower base fee plus a success fee on measured realized value, metered by the dated check-in records you hold. No value measured, no success fee — and the success fee rewards results only, never contingent on us recommending any particular path. We settle the exact investment together on a short call, once we understand what you're deciding.
