About

Consulting was due for a rebuild. So we built it.

Profit Pulse pairs structured decision intelligence with AI-accelerated analysis, applied by experienced people — to deliver better outcomes, faster, at a fraction of the cost of the famous firms.

Why we built this

A better way to make the calls that matter.

Traditional consulting was built for a different era — when analysis was scarce, expertise was rare, and clients paid for access to knowledge that lived inside specialist firms. That era is over.

AI now accelerates analysis to a degree that would have been unthinkable a decade ago. Yet most firms still operate as though nothing has changed: the same hourly rates, the same six-week diagnostics, the same hundred-page decks.

Profit Pulse was founded on a simple premise — there is a better way. Combine the rigor of a structured decision method with the speed of AI-accelerated analysis, applied by experienced people who know which questions actually matter. Deliver outcomes faster. Charge less. Earn the relationship through results, not billable hours.

What guides us

Five principles behind every engagement.

Evidence over opinion

Every claim should trace to specific evidence — not authority or reputation.

Structure over guesswork

Good decisions come from a disciplined process, not a charismatic slide deck.

Speed without shortcuts

AI lets us move fast while keeping the rigor a serious decision demands.

Honesty about uncertainty

We show how confident we are and where the risks sit — not just the answer we like.

Outcomes, not hours

We earn the relationship through results, priced on value — not billable time.

Leadership
